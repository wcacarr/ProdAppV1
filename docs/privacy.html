Tasuku — Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Tasuku does not collect, transmit or store any of your data anywhere except on your own phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server belonging to us. We cannot see anything you do in the app, because nothing reaches us.

What Tasuku is

Tasuku is a productivity app that lets you lock distracting apps on your own phone and earn timed access to them by completing tasks you set yourself.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to your phone's private app storage and never leaves it:

  • Your tasks — names, durations, times of day, and whether each is done
  • Your progress — XP, rank and day streak
  • Your settings — work hours, bedtime window, reminder and sound preferences
  • Your locked apps — which apps you chose to lock, and when any earned unlock expires
  • Photos you take as proof of a completed task — stored as image files in the app's own storage

Uninstalling Tasuku, or clearing its data in Android settings, permanently deletes all of it. There is no backup, and no way for us to recover it.

You can also erase everything from inside the app at any time, under Settings → Data → Erase all my data. That deletes your tasks, progress, settings and photo files, and unlocks every locked app.

Photos

Photos taken to prove a task is complete are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded, never shared, and are not accessible to other apps. Nobody, including us, can see them. They are deleted when you erase your data, uninstall the app, or clear its data.

Permissions, and why each is needed

Accessibility service

Tasuku uses Android's accessibility service to notice when an app you have locked is opened, so it can return you to the home screen. It reads only the package name of the app currently in the foreground. It does not read screen contents, text you type, passwords or messages, and it does not record or transmit anything.

Notification access

Used to show and control whatever media is playing on your phone in the app's player. It reads the current track's title, artist and playback position from the system media session. This is displayed only, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Camera

Used only when you take a photo to prove a task is complete, and only at the moment you choose to take one.

Notifications

Used to tell you when a task timer has finished, and to send optional reminders. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No push service is used, and no notification content leaves your phone.

Query all packages

Used to show you the apps installed on your phone so you can choose which to lock. The list is read on your device and is never transmitted.

Analytics and advertising

Tasuku contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking libraries of any kind.

Data sharing

We do not share your data, because we do not have it. Nothing is sold, transferred to third parties, or used for any purpose beyond running the app on your device.

Children

Tasuku is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
