# Privacy Policy — Tasuku

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

Tasuku is a productivity app that lets you lock distracting apps on your phone
and earn timed access to them by completing your own tasks.

## The short version

Tasuku does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data anywhere except on
your own phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server belonging to us.
We cannot see anything you do in the app, because nothing reaches us.

## What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to your phone's private app storage and never
leaves it:

- **Your tasks** — names, durations, times of day, and whether each is done
- **Your progress** — XP, rank and day streak
- **Your settings** — work hours, bedtime window, reminder and sound preferences
- **Your locked apps** — which apps you have chosen to lock, and when any
  purchased unlock expires
- **Photos you take as proof of a completed task** — stored as image files in
  the app's own storage

Uninstalling Tasuku, or clearing its data in Android settings, permanently
deletes all of it. There is no backup and no way for us to recover it.

You can also erase everything from inside the app at any time:
**Settings → Data → Erase all my data**. This deletes your tasks, progress,
settings and photo files, and unlocks every locked app.

## Photos

Photos taken to prove a task is complete are saved in the app's private storage
on your device. They are never uploaded, never shared, and are not accessible to
other apps. Nobody, including us, can see them. They are deleted when you erase
your data, delete the app, or clear its data.

## Permissions and why they are needed

**Accessibility service** — Tasuku uses Android's accessibility service to
notice when an app you have locked is opened, so it can return you to the home
screen. It reads only the package name of the app currently in the foreground.
It does not read screen contents, text you type, passwords, or messages, and it
does not record or transmit anything.

**Notification access** — used to show and control whatever media is playing on
your phone in the app's player dock. It reads the currently playing track's
title, artist and playback position from the system media session. This
information is displayed only and is never stored or sent anywhere.

**Camera** — used only when you take a photo to prove a task is complete, and
only at the moment you choose to take one.

**Notifications** — used to tell you when a task timer has finished and to send
optional reminders. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No
push service is used and no notification content leaves your phone.

**Query all packages** — used to show you the list of apps installed on your
phone so you can choose which to lock. The list is read on your device and is
never transmitted.

## Analytics and advertising

Tasuku contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no
third-party tracking libraries of any kind.

## Data sharing

We do not share your data, because we do not have it. No data is sold,
transferred to third parties, or used for any purpose beyond running the app on
your device.

## Children

Tasuku is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from anyone.
